Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how LUXA Ecosystem ("LUXA", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website, Telegram mini app, bot, and related services (collectively, the "Services"). By using the Services, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with the Services, we may collect:

  • Telegram account data provided by Telegram when you open our mini app or interact with our bot (such as your Telegram user ID, username, and profile photo).
  • Wallet addresses you connect or provide to interact with LUXA tokens, NFTs, or on-chain features.
  • Usage data, such as in-app activity, mining sessions, referrals, and progress within the app.
  • Technical data, such as IP address, device type, and browser information, collected automatically for security and anti-abuse purposes.
  • Communications you send us directly, for example via email or Telegram support.

We do not knowingly collect government ID documents, financial account numbers, or other sensitive personal data unless a specific feature explicitly requires it and you choose to provide it.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate and maintain the mini app, bot, and website
  • To track mining progress, rewards, referrals, and leaderboard rankings
  • To process wallet connections and on-chain transactions you initiate
  • To detect and prevent fraud, bots, and abuse of the reward system
  • To communicate with you about updates, support requests, or important changes
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations

4. On-Chain Data

LUXA operates on a public blockchain. Any transaction you make (transfers, NFT activity, wallet addresses) is recorded on a public, permanent ledger that anyone can view using our block explorer or a compatible third-party tool. This on-chain data is not controlled by us and cannot be deleted once confirmed.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited data with:

  • Service providers who help us operate the Services (e.g., hosting, database, analytics providers), under confidentiality obligations
  • Telegram, to the extent required for the mini app and bot to function
  • Authorities, if required by law, regulation, or valid legal process

6. Data Retention

We retain account and usage data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Services, prevent fraud, and comply with legal obligations. On-chain data, by its nature, is retained permanently on the blockchain and outside our control.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of the personal data we hold about you (excluding data that is permanently recorded on the blockchain). To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

9. Children's Privacy

The Services are not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the Services after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
