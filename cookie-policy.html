1. What Are Cookies and Local Storage?

Cookies are small text files that websites send to the user's device. Within the LUXA Ecosystem (luxa-1), the architecture relies primarily on the device's HTML5 LocalStorage to preserve full non-custodial sovereignty for the user.

2. Technical & Cryptographic Cookies (Strictly Necessary)

These elements are essential for the protocol to function correctly and cannot be disabled in our systems:

  • luxa_user_vaults: stores protected public addresses for your locally derived Spider Vaults.
  • luxa_cookie_consent: records the user's consent selection (Accept All or Essential Only).
  • Telegram WebApp Session: ensures cryptographic validation of the mining session and the 24-hour streak counter.

3. Radical Protection of Private Keys (Zero-Knowledge)

In line with the LUXA Protocol Constitution, no cookie or remote script can access your seed phrases (12/24 words) or private keys. Encryption (PBKDF2 with 210,000 iterations and AES-256-GCM) happens entirely on the client side. Validation servers and nodes do not retain credential traces.

4. No Third-Party Profiling

The LUXA Ecosystem does not sell browsing data, install advertising trackers, or profile transactions for marketing. All documentary validation for Mainnet migration (Sumsub) takes place through protected channels that comply with international legal requirements.
