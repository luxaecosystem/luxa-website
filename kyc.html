Identity Verification (KYC)

Protocol Framework: September 16, 2026 • luxa-1

1. Purpose of Identity Attestation

The LUXA Ecosystem distributes real economic value via passive in-app mining, community airdrops, and block rewards. To protect genuine community members and prevent automated bot swarms, click-farms, and Sybil attackers from depleting protocol liquidity, all balance migrations from the off-chain mining ledger to self-custodial on-chain Vaults require verification.

Notice on Migration: Early test claims inside the Telegram Mini App were unlocked strictly for sandbox validation. Full production settlement and unrestricted Cosmos L1 asset transfers require an approved KYC attestation bound to your sovereign luxa1... address.

2. Verification Infrastructure (Sumsub Powered)

LUXA Ecosystem SARL partners with Sumsub, a global compliance leader, to deliver secure, bank-grade biometric validation directly inside our Web3 workflows.

  • Document Authentication: Automated checks for national identity cards, international passports, or valid driver's licenses.
  • 3D Biometric Liveness: Fast, camera-based facial geometry check verifying that the claimant is a real living human.
  • Anti-Fraud Screening: Instant cross-checking against global watchlists, sanctioned jurisdictions, and duplicate account hashes.

3. The 3-Step Verification Flow

1. Connect Vault

Initialize your non-custodial Spider Vault with your 12-word recovery seed to derive your permanent Bech32 address.

2. Complete Sumsub Scan

Open the in-app KYC Portal, capture your official ID, and complete the instant 3D selfie check.

3. Unlock On-Chain Bridge

Once approved with a verified status, your account unlocks daily bridging of LUXA and SHARDS to the luxa-1 chain.

4. Zero On-Chain Data Exposure (Privacy by Design)

In full compliance with international data privacy legislation (GDPR and Moroccan Law No. 09-08), no personal identification documents, selfies, or legal names are ever stored or broadcast on the public blockchain.

The protocol validator only records a zero-knowledge cryptographic signature confirming that address luxa1... is owned by an attested individual. Raw personal data remains securely encrypted off-chain within Sumsub’s certified vault infrastructure.

5. Eligibility and Requirements

  • You must be at least 18 years of age.
  • You must hold a valid government-issued photo ID (not expired).
  • One person, one node: each legal identity may only be linked to a single master mining account. Multiple Telegram accounts attempting to verify with the same ID will be permanently flagged and forfeited.
