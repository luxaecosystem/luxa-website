Compliance & Security

Mainnet Settlement:
60 Days & Identity Check

Mining inside Telegram is free and open to everyone. Moving your balance onto the luxa-1 mainnet is earned: it takes proven commitment and a verified identity, which keeps bots and duplicate accounts out. Full guidelines in the KYC Portal.

  1. Initialize your vault

    Create or import a luxa1... Sovereign Vault and link it to your Telegram account.

  2. Reach 60 mining days

    60 validated daily mining sessions prove commitment. A progress bar in the app tracks your days.

  3. Verify your identity

    Complete the Sumsub check: 3D liveness and a biometric document scan.

  4. Enter the migration queue

    Your accumulated balance moves to your verified vault on the luxa-1 Cosmos ledger.

One Identity, All Vaults

Verification belongs to your Telegram account, not to a single address. Once approved, every vault you manage (up to 10) inherits the verified status.

Anti-Sybil Defense

Bots, fake clickers, duplicate accounts and coordinated farms are detected and banned automatically, and the reason is kept in an audit log.

Verifiable Settlement Proof

Each payout produces a verifiable document seal containing your Telegram ID, sender Treasury hash, and timestamped block height.

Strict Data Protection

Identity records are processed according to data privacy legislation and are never revealed or stored publicly on the open blockchain ledger.
