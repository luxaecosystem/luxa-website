The Vision

Built for the People,
Not the Privileged

LUXA was born from a simple idea: anyone with a smartphone and a Telegram account should be able to take part in the crypto world.

Zero Barriers

No KYC, no gas fees, no complex wallets. Just open Telegram and start mining instantly.

Community First

The value comes from the size and commitment of our community, not artificial scarcity.

True Ownership

Non-custodial wallet with BIP-39 seed phrase. Your keys, your coins. Always.

Transparent Economy

All parameters are visible and adjustable to keep the ecosystem sustainable long-term.

02

Secure Non-Custodial Wallet

Your 12-word seed phrase is generated and encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM. The server never sees it. True zero-knowledge security.

  • BIP-39 standard seed phrase
  • AES-256-GCM encryption
  • PIN protection with PBKDF2
03

P2P NFT Marketplace

Collect unique "Hero" NFTs with different rarities that boost your mining rate. Trade them directly with other users — no intermediaries, zero fees.

  • Collectible Hero NFTs
  • Direct user-to-user trading
  • Rarity-based mining boosts
