3. Dynamic Difficulty & The 7,000,000 Pioneer Absorption Proof

If the protocol distributed a fixed reward per mining session, an influx of 7,000,000 pioneers would deplete the entire 500,000,000 LUXA reserve in a matter of months. LUXA resolves this through an autonomous difficulty balancing algorithm inspired by Bitcoin’s hash difficulty adjustment, adapted for social mobile computing nodes:

Daily_Rewardi = Daily_Network_Budget × [ Mfibo, i / Σ Mfibo, active ]

Where Mfibo, i represents the active participant's Fibonacci streak multiplier (1.00x to 3.62x), and the denominator represents the aggregate harmonic weight of all valid nodes mining that day.

Mathematical Outcome for 7 Million Pioneers

  • Lifetime Pioneer Allocation: 500,000,000 LUXA ÷ 7,000,000 Pioneers = ~71.43 LUXA average lifetime yield per active node across the full 10-year curve.
  • Daily Scarcity at Scale: In Year 1 (~684,931 LUXA daily budget), if 7,000,000 active nodes compete simultaneously, the base unboosted participant receives approximately ~0.07 LUXA per 24h session.
  • Infinite Network Scalability: If participation surges past 10 million or 20 million miners, the 500M reserve cannot be breached. The formula simply divides the daily budget among more participants, driving organic micro-scarcity down to individual uluxa units without causing protocol inflation.
